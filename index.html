HOW TO PLAY

SEASON MODE: Play an 8-game regular season (Week 1 = Corky Kell at Mercedes-Benz). Win your region to qualify for playoffs.

PLAYOFFS: 32-team bracket based on power rankings. Win 5 games to claim the State Championship at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

GAMEPLAY: Old-school controls. Arrow Keys/WASD move your ball carrier. Hold SPACE for a TURBO burst of speed (watch the meter — it drains fast and refills when you let go). Tap SHIFT near a defender to JUKE past him — costs some turbo meter and briefly stuns whoever you shook. On pass plays your receiver runs the route automatically; press SPACE or tap the field to fire the ball to him the instant he's open. On 4th down: Go for it, Field Goal, or Punt.

RECRUITING: Use Dynasty Points to sign Georgia prospects and build your program rating over multiple seasons.
